# Privacy Policy for Color Picker Chrome Extension

**Product:** Color Picker Chrome Extension  
**Developer:** Prahlad Inala  
**Last updated:** November 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 2026

Color Picker ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

Color Picker does NOT collect, transmit, store on external servers, or sell any personal information or browsing data. All extension operations occur 100% locally on your computer.

## 2. Permissions Used

The Extension requests only the minimum necessary browser permissions to function as a color utility:

- **Storage (`chrome.storage.local`):** Used exclusively to store your picked color history locally on your device so your palette persists across browser sessions.
- **Clipboard (`clipboardWrite`):** Used solely to copy selected HEX color codes to your system clipboard when you click a color swatch.

## 3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any user data to third parties under any circumstances. All extension operations occur 100% locally on your computer.

## 4. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at [prahladjngp@gmail.com](mailto:prahladjngp@gmail.com).
