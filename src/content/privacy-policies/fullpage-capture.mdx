# Privacy Policy for FullPage Capture

**Product:** FullPage Capture - Smart Screenshot & Editor  
**Developer:** Prahlad Inala  
**Last updated:** November 8, 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 8, 2026

FullPage Capture - Smart Screenshot & Editor ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains our data practices.

---

## 1. 100% Client-Side Architecture

- **No Remote Servers:** The extension operates entirely within your local browser. There are no backend servers or cloud databases operated by us.
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, transmit, sell, or rent your personal information, IP addresses, browsing history, form submissions, passwords, or cookies.
- **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not include telemetry scripts, Google Analytics, or tracking SDKs.
- **No Account Registration:** No sign-up, login, or email address required.
- **Local Storage Only:** All captured screenshots and annotations are stored exclusively in your browser’s local IndexedDB and Chrome Storage. They never leave your device unless you choose to export them.

---

## 2. Permissions Justification

The extension requests only the minimum Chrome permissions required to perform screenshot captures and local file exports:

- **`activeTab`:** Grants temporary access to the active webpage when you invoke a capture action (popup click, hotkey, or context menu) to measure dimensions and capture visible pixels.
- **`scripting`:** Injects scrolling helpers to systematically scroll down and stitch full-page screenshots, and to display the element selection overlay.
- **`storage`:** Stores local user preferences (format, delay, filename template, download folder).
- **`downloads`:** Saves exported screenshots, PDFs, or ZIP archives directly to your local computer upon request.
- **`contextMenus`:** Creates right-click menu shortcuts ("Capture Full Webpage", "Capture Visible Area", "Capture Selected Area") for instant access.
- **`offscreen`:** Used for Canvas pixel stitching and thumbnail generation in compliance with Manifest V3 background service worker guidelines.
- **`host_permissions` (`http://*/*`, `https://*/*`):** Required by Chrome Manifest V3 `chrome.scripting` to coordinate scrolling on webpages the user chooses to screenshot. The extension never inspects, reads, or transmits webpage text or network traffic.

---

## 3. Data Storage & User Control

All screenshots are saved locally in the browser's IndexedDB. You maintain unilateral control over your captures:

- You can delete individual captures or clear your entire history at any time from the Screenshot Gallery.
- You can configure automatic retention pruning (e.g. 7 or 30 days) in Options.
- Uninstalling the extension automatically purges all locally stored IndexedDB records and settings from your browser.
