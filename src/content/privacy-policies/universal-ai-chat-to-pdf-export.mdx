# Privacy Policy for Universal AI Chat PDF Exporter

Last updated: October 2026

Universal AI Chat PDF Exporter ("the Extension") is committed to protecting user privacy.

## 1. Data Collection & Processing

The Extension operates 100% locally on your device. When you trigger an export, the Extension temporarily accesses the text, code blocks, diagrams, and formatting in the active AI conversation tab solely to compile your requested document (PDF, Markdown, JSON, or EPUB).

## 2. Zero Data Transmission

The Extension does NOT collect, track, store, or transmit your conversation text, prompts, personal data, or browsing activity to any external server, third party, or cloud service. All processing occurs in local browser memory.

## 3. Local Settings Storage

The Extension uses `chrome.storage.local` exclusively on your device to remember your UI preferences (e.g., chosen theme, paper size, code formatting style, and floating button screen position).

## 4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties.

## 5. Contact

For any privacy questions or inquiries regarding this extension, please contact the developer via the official support link on the Chrome Web Store.
