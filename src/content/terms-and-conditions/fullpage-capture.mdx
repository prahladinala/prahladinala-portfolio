## 1. Description of the Tool

FullPage Capture is a productivity tool for Chrome that enables scrolling webpage screenshots, visible area capture, canvas-based annotations (drawing, text, shapes, blur, highlights), and exporting captures to PNG, JPEG, PDF, or ZIP files directly within the local browser runtime.
